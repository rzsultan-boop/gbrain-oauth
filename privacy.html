Privacy policy

gbrain (personal) is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, with the owner's own Google account. Data read from Google (Gmail, Calendar, Contacts) is stored only on that computer. It is not sold, shared, transferred to third parties, or used for advertising or model training. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com.

Google user data handling follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
